About me

Practice first. Then the classroom.

I’m a marketing professional and Digital Marketing Instructor with an MBA in Marketing from the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport. Over eight years I have worked in marketing across Egypt and the Gulf, and I have trained around 400 learners along the way.

My work has covered social media, advertising campaigns, brand communication and team leadership, across industries including medical, beauty and cosmetics, real estate, construction, education and e-commerce. In the Gulf I have worked on markets such as Kuwait and Saudi Arabia.

That practical background shapes how I teach. I connect marketing concepts to real business cases through assignments, activities and case studies, so learners can apply what they learn.

I am now building on my MBA toward an academic career in Marketing and Business Administration, while continuing to lead marketing in the Gulf and train the next generation of marketers.
